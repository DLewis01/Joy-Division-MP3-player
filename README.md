# Joy-Division-MP3-player
You've probably worn the shirt without knowing it has the screams of a dying star on it, PSR B1919+21, in the constellation Vulpecula.

Joy Divisions Guitarist Bernard Sumner found the graphic in the Cambridge Encyclopaedia of Astronomy (and previously in Scientific American), and Peter Saville subsequently inverted the colours and created the famous white-on-black album sleeve.

How famous? The PM wears it, I wear it, I've brought the single and 12 inch more times than I can remember. 

I was annoyed I couldn't get cross site web loading working for 'Rangoon', but this demonstrates that if site has a good CORS policy, you can stream it. This is streaming from archive.org.

Not only is the player in the style of the album sleeve, but it auto loads 'Love will tears us part' if you just press play, or you can play the entire album. 

Then go and stream the Docu-movie 'Control' to see how Joy Division formed, then Stream 24 hour party People to see the insanity of that time.
